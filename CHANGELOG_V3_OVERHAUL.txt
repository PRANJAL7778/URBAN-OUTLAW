URBAN OUTLAW V3 — GAMEPLAY, CHARACTER ANIMATION & UI OVERHAUL
===========================================================================
This is an upgrade of the working V3 build, not a rebuild. City, cars, missions,
saving, audio, controls and assets are all still there; the changes below are
additive or replace specific broken pieces.

The untouched pre-overhaul build is archived at:
  backup/urban_outlaw_v3_working_build.zip      (git tag: v3-working-baseline)
and the original V2/V3 archive is still at backup/urban_outlaw_v3_project.zip.


1. LANDSCAPE LAYOUT, SAFE AREAS, ROTATE HINT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
* New src/ui.css — landscape-first interface, one stylesheet, no external fonts.
* All HUD edges now use env(safe-area-inset-*) with 14 px fallbacks, so the
  joystick, action buttons, pause button and speed readout sit clear of Android
  gesture navigation and rounded corners.
* Portrait is supported (the phone, menu and HUD re-flow) and a dismissible
  "rotate for the full view" hint appears in portrait instead of blocking play.
* Joystick kept at 128 px so its centre maths still matches the old layout;
  the knob is a DOM SVG circle, unchanged in behaviour.


2. THREE SEPARATE CAMERA SYSTEMS  (src/camera.js — new)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
* On foot: smooth follow from behind/above, touch-drag to orbit, pitch clamped
  0.10–1.05 rad, distance clamped 2.2–6.5 m, sensitivity from Settings, gentle
  auto-align behind the hero only after 1.1 s without a drag.
* Car: chase camera that trails the heading (rate rises with speed), shows the
  road ahead by looking 6.5–11.5 m forward, and grows 6.2 → 7.7 m with speed.
* Motorcycle: separate closer chase camera (5.0 → 6.0 m), looks further ahead,
  and never rolls the horizon (camera.up is always world up, tested).
* Anti-clipping: the distance — not the position — is smoothed, so the lens always
  sits on the ray that was just tested against the building boxes; a wall can pull
  the camera in immediately (no see-through) and it eases back out at 4.5/s.
* Mode switches snap once (camRig.jump) instead of flying across the map.


3. CHARACTER ARMS  (src/ik.js + src/pose.js — new)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
The rig was measured first, not guessed:
  left arm 0.50 m, right arm 0.56 m, shoulders at x +0.13 / -0.25, bone length
  axis -Y, elbows flexing correctly in every clip.
So the problem was not placement, it was that the shared ragdoll clips assume a
symmetric rig. The fix is a procedural arm pass for the walking hero:
* two-bone IK from the real shoulder positions, with every offset expressed as a
  fraction of that arm's own length, so a 0.50 m arm and a 0.56 m arm swing the
  same way (measured: identical 0.720 swing range on both sides);
* per-state poses: idle (small breathing swing), run, sprint, and a symmetric
  two-arms-up air pose (no alternation while airborne);
* elbows keep a human angle in every state, hands stay outside the torso;
* hands are re-oriented so the fingers continue the forearm (no twisted wrist).
Legs, spine, landing squash and the ragdoll physics are untouched.


4. MOTORCYCLE RIDING POSE  (src/pose.js)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The bike is measured from its own geometry at mount time: handlebar grips (in
  the fork's own space, so they follow the steering), foot pegs, and the seat
  (40% of the wheelbase behind the front axle, at the top surface).
* The rider is a SkeletonUtils clone with its own skeleton, so the hero's model
  is never reparented (the old code moved the shared scene into the bike).
* Pose: pelvis on the seat (measured seat + 0.02 m/0.09 m forward), both hands
  solved onto the measured grips, fingers wrapped across the bar, feet on the
  pegs with the ankle 0.075 m above them, torso lean calibrated once so the arms
  actually reach the low bars (0.64 rad here, ≤ 5% arm extension).
* Hands follow the fork as it steers (measured 0 mm error at rest, ≤ 3 cm at full
  lock), and the chest rotates with the steering like a real rider.
* Mount/dismount unchanged in behaviour; the rider is disposed with its skeleton
  on dismount (no leak).


5. MAIN MENU  (src/ui.js — new)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cinematic landscape menu over the live city: title block, and six items —
Continue, New Game, Missions, Garage (coming soon), Smartphone, Settings.
* Continue/Missions stay disabled until the city, both vehicles and the hero are
  actually loaded (the button says "loading world…" and shows the boot status).
* New Game asks for confirmation; best score and courier records are kept.
* Settings are real: sound, vibration, camera sensitivity, and a graphics preset
  (Auto / Low / High) that drives pixel ratio and shadows live.
* Credits screen keeps the CC BY 4.0 attribution and the diagnostics dump.


6. IN-GAME SMARTPHONE  (src/ui.js)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Opens from the HUD PHONE button or the menu; closes with ✕, Escape or a tap
outside, with a scale/fade animation. Apps: Map, Missions, Contacts (coming
soon), Garage (coming soon), Settings, Profile.
* Map: live top-down canvas of the blocks, roads, parked vehicles, objective and
  the player arrow, refreshed 2.5×/s while open.
* Missions: real courier start/drop actions and the delivery-run status.
* Settings: same controls as the pause menu, kept in sync.
* Profile: tokens, best score, courier runs, session time.
* While the phone is open, movement, jump, drag-look and vehicle throttle are
  blocked and the car brakes, so the player cannot drive off behind the panel.
  The world keeps simulating (no hard freeze).


7. BOOT AND FIRST-SCREEN FIXES  (found by actually executing main.js headlessly)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Fixed a boot-order crash: the graphics preset was applied before its state
  variable existed, so the module threw during start-up and the game never began.
  This was the one defect that would have shown as "black screen" on a phone.
* The main menu is now shown by the UI itself at boot. Before this the menu only
  appeared after the player had already left it, so a first visit could show an
  empty screen while the city loaded behind it.
* Settings opened from the menu now pauses the game and closing it resumes —
  previously it could leave the simulation frozen with no way back.
* The phone now also cancels a joystick push in the same frame it opens, so the
  hero cannot coast a few metres while the panel is being read.

KNOWN LIMITS (see TEST_REPORT_V3_OVERHAUL.md)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
* No browser or real-Android run was possible in this environment; everything
  below was verified headlessly. Please check the feel on the phone.
* The scan rig itself is asymmetric (one arm is 6 cm longer); the animation is
  equalised, the mesh is not re-rigged.
* At a wall corner the walking camera can pull in by up to ~2.5 m in one frame
  (it would rather move than show through the wall). Car and bike cameras never
  snap.
* Contacts and Garage are deliberately marked coming soon instead of shipping
  empty screens.
